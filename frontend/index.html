How CivicLens Works

📸

1. Snap & Pin

Citizens submit photos and drop an exact GPS pin in English or ಕನ್ನಡ (Kannada).

🧠

2. AI Multilingual Triage

Computer vision detects defects and spatial algorithms group duplicate reports into one canonical incident.

✅

3. Track & Verify

Municipal crews repair the hazard and upload before-and-after photographic evidence with public transparency.
