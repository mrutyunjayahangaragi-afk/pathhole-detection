How CivicLens Works

1. Snap & Pin

Citizens capture geotagged photos and drop an exact GPS pin in English or ಕನ್ನಡ (Kannada).

2. AI Multilingual Triage

Computer vision detects defects and spatial clustering groups duplicate reports into one canonical incident.

3. Track & Verify

Municipal crews repair the hazard and upload before-and-after photographic evidence with public transparency.
